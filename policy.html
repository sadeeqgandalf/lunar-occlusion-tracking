What this lab simplifies

  • One rock and a flat ground; the rover sees the rock's position and size, not camera pixels.
  • The expert is a geometric planner, not a human driver.
  • Unicycle motion with no slip; the policy re-plans every 1 s and runs 4 of its 8 actions.
  • Action tokens use FAST's cosine transform without its byte-pair encoding.
